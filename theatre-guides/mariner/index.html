Tulip Axiality

6 tulip options
P
Polyaxial65° conical angulation, full multi-axis. Extended-reduction tower: +15mm reduction.
D
Deformity65° in three planes, reduced motion medial/lateral for distraction techniques. Max angulation for optimal rod capture. Extended-reduction tower: +15mm.
T
Trauma65° in three planes, reduced motion cephalad/caudal for superior distraction strength.
No screw in the Mariner platform is monoaxial — P/D/T are all angulating; D/T are motion-limited rather than free multi-axis. Threaded modular head-to-shank connection (self-tapping cutting flute, T30 drive) adds implant strength at assembly.

Indications

summary only

Immobilisation and stabilisation of thoracic, lumbar and sacral spinal segments as an adjunct to fusion — DDD, spondylolisthesis, trauma, spinal stenosis, deformity/curvature, tumour, and pseudarthrosis or failed previous fusion, in skeletally mature patients.

This is a summary for theatre reference only — see the full Instructions for Use for indications, contraindications and warnings before use.

Standard MIS Technique

BrainLab/AIRO-navigated, percutaneous
1

Access & Targeting

Drill guide, docked on the pedicle trajectory under navigation guidance — Fig. 2
1
Surgical approach — patient prone; BrainLab/AIRO navigation platform registered and level confirmed; incision made over the planned trajectory.
2
Target & access pedicle — BrainLab drill guide assembled with a long nut on the end and a trocar (pointed obturator tip) inserted through it; positioned directly onto the planned trajectory (~2cm incision), start point confirmed on the navigation platform.
3
Trocar malleted by hand into the bone to establish a secure starting position; trocar removed and drill inserted through the guide to drill to the planned depth, confirmed on the navigation platform.
4
Advance guidewire through the navigated drill guide into the vertebral body by hand pressure; remove the drill guide, leaving the guidewire secured.
5
Dilator placement — Dilator 1 over the guidewire, docked on the posterior elements; Dilator 2 advanced over Dilator 1 (insulated, used to tap through); Dilator 3 advanced over Dilator 2 (insulated, used for screw placement).
2

Pedicle Prep & Screw Placement

6
Pedicle preparation — MIS Cannulated Navigation Tap matching the planned screw diameter; tap to depth, confirmed on the navigation platform.Note: taps are line-to-line, e.g. a 5.5mm tap is 5.5mm OD
Step 7 — choose reduction platform
7aExtended Tabs

Shank threaded into an Extended Tab Tulip until polyaxial; navigated screwdriver locked to implant and inserted over the guidewire through Dilator 3.

7bMIS Towers

MIS Tower snapped onto the tulip in its caddy first; shank + tulip + tower assembled, then driven over the guidewire through Dilator 3 with the navigated screwdriver.

3

Rod Measurement & Insertion

Constrained rod inserter aligning with the screw extensions — Fig. 35
7
Tulip exchange (optional) — alignment centring guide + head turner swap tulip type in situ without removing the screw.
8
Rod measurement — Head Adjuster aligns tulips for rod passage; Rod Measurement Caliper reads cephalad-to-caudal tulip. Round up to the nearest 5mm if between sizes; re-measure after any extra contouring.
Step 9 — choose rod delivery
9aPercutaneous

MIS Percutaneous Rod Inserter locks the rod (green→red window) and delivers it through the screw extensions in 60° increments.

9bConstrained / Direct

Wiltse Rod Inserter carries a constrained rod from its caddy; each end enters the screw extensions, then the inserter rotates 90° to seat the rod.

4

Set Screw, Reduction & Final Tighten

Final tightening — T-handle to an audible click, 85 in-lb — Fig. 51
10
Set screw insertion — gold-knob driver locks onto the set screw in its caddy and delivers it into the screw extension; fully seated once the shaft laser mark is covered.
11
Reduction (optional) — Tower Reducer engages the MIS Tower, preset to the desired amount (max 40mm); reduce by hand or with the Reduction Driver until the green ring shows fully reduced, then flip the driver 180° to deliver the set screw.
12
Final tightening — Extended Tab Body or Dual-End Counter Torque stabilises the tulip/tower; torque-limiting T-handle turned to an audible click.85 in-lb final torque
5

Compression / Distraction (optional)

Free Fulcrum with compressor/distractor at skin level — Fig. 58
13aTower-based

CD Tower/Extended Tab Arm + CD Main Arm span the adjacent level; CD Driver loosens the set screw, compressor/distractor adjusts, then re-tighten and final-tighten per Step 12.

13bFree Fulcrum

Free Fulcrum placed over the target segment at skin level; CD Driver loosens the set screw, compressor/distractor adjusts, then re-tighten and final-tighten per Step 12.

6

Implant Removal

Tab Clipper releasing the extended tab from the tulip — Fig. 59
14aTulip Removal

Tab Clipper jaws engage the extended tabs; squeeze until the proximal ring separates, then remove medially/laterally. Repeat opposite side.

14bTower Removal

Tower Remover onto the MIS Tower; downward pressure rotates the gold dial Ready→Remove, then lift tower and remover together.

Hardware removal: Shank Depth Adjuster backs out set screws and shanks; rod lifts out with the Direct Delivery (Wiltse) Rod Inserter.

Related Mariner Systems

companion guides on file
Mariner MIS WayfinderScrew delivery
  • All-in-one k-wireless screw delivery system — impaction-driven stylet replaces the targeting needle/dilator/tap sequence, then the Mariner MIS screw drives directly over the stylet.
  • Stylet depth control 3–25mm in 5mm increments; accommodates screw lengths 30–60mm; diamond or bevel stylet tips.
  • Compatible with standard, navigated (FLASH™ 7D) and shank drivers; single-level tray.
Mariner OutriggerRevision & extension
  • Rod-to-rod connector system for extending an existing fusion construct or revising around retained hardware — 5.5 & 6.0mm rod combinations.
  • Box Chisel / C-Chisel, Rasp and Rongeur clear prior fusion mass and gain clearance on the implanted rod before the Outrigger connector is placed and provisionally tightened.
  • Final tightening via T-25 (aqua set screws) or T-30 (gold set screws) torque driver with counter torque — 85 in-lb, audible click.
Both are separate SeaSpine product lines used alongside core Mariner MIS implants — not covered step-by-step here. A full in-app guide for either can be built on request.
